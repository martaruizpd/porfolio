About me

Bio

Hi, I'm Marta! :)

I'm a product designer with 10+ years of experience, based in Spain. For 5+ years I was embedded in Kroger's product org via Empathy.co, designing the search and ads experiences that tens of millions of people use for their grocery shopping.

I'm happiest when I'm close to the people I design for. Research and testing are at the core of my work, from moderated and unmoderated studies, to A/B tests and accessibility evaluation, and I'm comfortable explaining my design decisions to senior stakeholders up to Director/VP level.

I like to get involved early: doing research, gathering requirements with the team, designing wireframes, high-fidelity screens and workflows, keeping everyone up to date as things change, and seeing an initiative through to launch.

Outside design, I love spending time with my family and doing sport. I've been running for many years now, mostly 10Ks and half marathons. Running is how I recharge my batteries.
