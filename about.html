About me

Bio

Hi, I'm Marta! :)

I'm a product designer with 10+ years of experience. For 5+ years I was embedded in Kroger's product teams, designing the search and ads experiences that tens of millions of people use for their grocery shopping.

I'm happiest when I'm close to the people I design for. Research and testing are at the heart of what I do, from moderated and unmoderated usability studies to A/B tests and accessibility evaluations.

I like working across the whole product cycle: doing research and competitive analysis, finding product opportunities, defining requirements and designing wireframes, workflows and high-fidelity screens to test with users and in A/B tests. I work closely with cross-functional teams, and follow each initiative through to launch and its short- and long-term impact. I love turning findings into insights the team can act on, and I'm comfortable presenting my decisions to senior stakeholders, up to Director and VP level.

Outside design, I love spending time with my family and staying active. I've been running for many years, mostly 10Ks and half marathons. It's how I recharge.
