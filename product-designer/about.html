Bio

10+ years in design, 5+ at the intersection of search and commerce.

I'm a Product Designer with 10+ years of experience, specialising in complex search and e-commerce at scale. For the past 5+ years I was embedded in Kroger's product org via Empathy.co — working remotely from Spain with US-based teams — designing search and monetisation experiences for tens of millions of customers.

User research and testing are at the core of my work (moderated and unmoderated studies, A/B tests, accessibility evaluation), and I'm comfortable communicating design decisions to senior stakeholders up to Director/VP level. The work I'm most proud of sits at the intersection of clear user problems and real business impact.

I like to get involved early: gathering requirements with the team, keeping everyone up to date as things change, and seeing an initiative through to launch, even when that means stepping outside my usual role.

Based in Spain. Experienced collaborating across timezones with US-based teams. Open to product design roles, remote from Spain with UK, EU or US teams, and happy to travel.
