Sponsored Products & Video Ad Formats

Ad formats that stand out on web and mobile, tested with users, A/B tests and a screen-reader study — with more impressions and add-to-carts for Kroger and its brands.

Company Kroger (via Empathy.co)
Period 2022–2026
My role Sole Product Designer, Monetisation Ads
Platform Web, iOS & Android

Result

More impressions and add-to-carts, for Kroger and for brands.

Platforms shipped: web, iOS, Android

Opportunity

Competitors were reaching more shoppers. Kroger's ads needed to stand out.

The opportunity came from what competitors were doing: reach more customers and make ads catch their attention. Kroger already had some featured carousels live, but their layout needed rethinking to make sense on both web and mobile.

My role

Sole designer, owning the project end to end.

  • Ran the full design process: research, prototyping, user testing and A/B testing, then turned the test results into insights.
  • Made decisions with the Product Manager and Design Manager, grounded in those insights.
  • Proposed and ran accessibility testing, including a study with a screen-reader user.

I proposed accessibility testing and ran a study with a screen-reader user. The feedback was very good, with impact for users and for the business.

What I designed

Ad formats built from what shoppers already know.

  • Layouts from existing components. I combined components already on the site that weren't sponsored features, so the new formats stay consistent with the rest of the experience.
  • A "Featured" label on video and carousels. I added the label to the video and carousel formats so sponsored content is clear while still getting attention.
  • Better carousels on web and mobile. I enhanced the featured carousels already live, reworking the layout so it makes sense on both.

Outcome

Good for shoppers, good for brands.

Impressions and add-to-carts increased for Kroger and for the brands advertising. The screen-reader study showed the formats also worked for users who rely on assistive technology.
