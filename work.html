More of my work

The case studies above are a selection of my most relevant work. Other projects I'm proud of:

Accessibility

Responsive, accessible ad formats

Ads used to be a single flat image, with the text baked in. Now the text is part of the page, so ads adapt to every screen size and screen readers can read them.

Homepage

Sponsored content in homepage campaigns

Kroger often runs campaigns in the homepage hero space. We opened that space to brands, fitting sponsored ads into those campaigns, with a huge impact.

Pricing

Dynamic price tags in ads

Brands can pull a product's price straight from its product card in the catalogue and show it on top of their ad.
